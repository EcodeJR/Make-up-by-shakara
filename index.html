The Artist

Meet Blessing Teni

"Every face is a passport — I just help it travel further."

Since 2014, Blessing has built a name in Abuja for glam that photographs as well as it feels. What started as studio sessions on Ali Muhammad Zarah Street has grown into a destination practice — flying out for weddings in France and Egypt, and building a loyal client base across the Nigerian diaspora in Canada and Europe. Every booking starts with skin prep, not products, and ends with a look built to hold through twelve hours of dancing.
